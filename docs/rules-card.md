# Rules card

The kit's rules on one page. The full text is in `instructions/CLAUDE.md` and its rules; your organisation's managed
settings outrank all of it.

## Never (Red Lines)
- Paste a credential value into a file, note or commit: refer to it by name.
- Fabricate or backdate a document or record.

## Hard stop rule
When a permission rule, proxy or policy denies a step: show the denial word for word and stop that step; never retry
it in another shape. The ask for IT is in `docs/admin-asks.md`.

## Confirm first
Irreversible deletions and real money. Force pushes and repository or release deletes ask through the permission
rules (`settings/SETTINGS.md`).

## Files
- Never bulk-read or index online-only OneDrive files; never bulk move or delete in a synced folder.

## Working habits
- Evidence over claims: a green check is not proof; open the result.
- Big or risky work: a builder, an attacker on a different model, and a final verifier.
- Smallest change that fully solves the problem, once you understand it.
- Save to the knowledge base and write a handoff before a session ends.

## Where to look
How-tos `docs/howto/README.md` · fixes by symptom `docs/troubleshooting.md` · gaps `docs/known-limits.md` · new
laptop and offboarding `docs/runbooks.md`.
