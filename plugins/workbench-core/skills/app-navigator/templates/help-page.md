# Reports

Route: /reports
Who can open it: member, admin

## What it is
The list of every report in your workspace, newest first, with its owner and status.

## What you can do here
- Open a report to read it (each report has its own page; there is no fixed link to one report).
- Filter by owner, status or date with the bar above the list.
- Export the visible list as CSV with **Export** (admins only).
- Start a new report with **New report**; the editor opens at [New report](/reports/new).
