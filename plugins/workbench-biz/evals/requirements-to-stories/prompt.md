Here are notes from the kickoff of Project Heron, an internal tool for booking meeting rooms at Example Co. Please turn them into user stories with acceptance criteria.

- Staff can search for a free room by date, time and number of people.
- A booking lasts up to 4 hours. Managers may extend it; nobody said how.
- The room owner gets an email when a booking is cancelled.
- It must work on phones. Pages should load fast.
- Booking history is kept; the retention period is not decided.
