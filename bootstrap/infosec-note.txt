Before the first full run, tell your security team what this does. The workbench builds a local search index of the document libraries it finds that you can already open, and, only if you turn capture or ingest on, saves read-only Markdown copies of intranet pages, shared files, your own mail and meeting transcripts. The index stays on this machine, in your local app-data folder; copies stay on this machine or in your organisation's own work OneDrive, never in a personal account. Copies are made at a human pace with an hourly cap, files with a sensitivity label are skipped unless you opt in, and online-only files are not downloaded just to be indexed. Captured copies stored in the work cloud fall under your organisation's retention, DLP and eDiscovery policies. AI digests of your mail and meetings run through your own approved Claude sign-in, and meeting transcripts can contain the speech of people outside your organisation. You can remove it all at any time: the find skill's purge removes the index, and capture purge removes the captured copies.
